**[0:00–0:12] Hook** *(presenter close-up)*
> Imagine you're an astronaut on your way to Mars, and you suddenly feel your heart racing.
> You call Earth for help, and your message takes twenty minutes to arrive. The answer takes twenty more.

**[0:12–0:27] Problem** *(optional: text on screen listing the risks)*
> On long missions, the crew faces serious health risks: bone loss, muscle atrophy, vision damage,
> radiation, poor sleep and stress. But today, most medical support still depends on doctors on the ground.
> On Mars, that's simply too slow.

**[0:27–0:35] Solution**
> That's why we're building **UnoSyn**: an onboard health system that lets the crew take care of themselves.

**[0:35–1:10] How it works** *(optional: simple mockup or icons for each point)*
> It has four parts.
> First, a **crew dashboard**. Every astronaut gets a live health score out of a hundred, based on heart rate,
> oxygen level, temperature and sleep.
> Second, **machine learning**. The system learns each astronaut's normal patterns and flags anything unusual
> early, like an abnormal heart rate for three hours, before it becomes an emergency.
> Third, an **AI medical assistant** that guides the crew through first aid step by step, without waiting for Earth.
> And fourth, a **smart pharmacy** that tracks every medicine and warns when stock is low or when radiation
> has shortened a drug's shelf life.

**[1:10–1:20] Tech + data**
> We'll build it with Python, FastAPI, scikit-learn and React, grounded in NASA's open science data.

**[1:20–1:30] Close** *(the whole team in frame)*
> We're a team of computer science students from the Egyptian Chinese University.
> UnoSyn: because on Mars, help can't be twenty minutes away.

## Recording tips
- Landscape, at eye level, with a window or lamp in front of you (not behind).
- Use a quiet room; a phone's earphones mic is better than the built-in mic from a distance.
- You don't need to memorize it: put the text on a laptop just behind the phone and read slowly.
- Do 3–4 takes and pick the best. Add captions (CapCut does it automatically).
- Check the form's exact video length limit before you export.
